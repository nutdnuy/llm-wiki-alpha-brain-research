# Alpha Research Wiki

Start with [Research Index](Research.md).

Evidence flows through Sources, Hypotheses, Candidates, Experiments and
Syntheses under 02-Wiki. Source and experiment artifacts live in 01-Raw.
Sources and hypotheses are shared by BRAIN and Python/Qlib. Implementations
and results stay separate by backend, with exact settings and evidence.
Read [Contract](../04-Schema/Contract.md) before creating records.
Copy the matching note template from `04-Schema/Templates/` and fill it from
evidence. Templates are scaffolds; they do not establish any empirical result.
Operations are recorded in [Log](../03-Logs/Log.md).
