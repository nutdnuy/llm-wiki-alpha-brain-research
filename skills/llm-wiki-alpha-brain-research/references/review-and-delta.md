# Review and evidence changes

## Research review

Review source fidelity, economic logic and experiment methodology separately.
An evidence reviewer reads the cited source/artifact, checks its identity,
numbers, units, dates and scope. A methodology reviewer checks timing,
comparison validity, costs, selection history and the strongest competing
explanation. Use available specialist roles only when delegation is authorized;
do not invent reviewer identities or appoint a permanent agent.

Self-checking can improve a draft but does not satisfy independent review.
Keep status draft until a different reviewer actually checks the required
evidence and records reviewed_by and review_evidence. A real statistical
result can be recorded before review; its note review state remains separate.

Check backend identity and exact candidate/settings match before interpreting
a result. `hard-pass` needs a matching evidenced pass, and means only the
recorded criteria passed. Lint cannot verify whether those criteria were
scientifically adequate or whether a claimed independent review happened.

## Delta report

For "what changed", inspect notes and experiments added or changed since the
requested date or a verified prior report. When no baseline exists, state the
chosen window. Follow references to the shared hypotheses and raw evidence;
do not use file modification time alone as an observation date.

Classify each change as strengthens, weakens, contradicts, kill criterion
met, or inconclusive. Cite the source/claim/locator or experiment/artifact,
name the exact hypothesis point affected, and state uncertainty. A pending
claim may raise a concern but cannot alone establish that a kill condition
was met. Different universes or cost models may explain a disagreement;
retain both results and test the explanation.

Default output is a concise report. If persistence is requested, write a new
`03-Logs/Delta/<run-id>.md` containing:

| Hypothesis | New evidence | Backend / actual scope | Effect | Next check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not modify candidate snapshots or promote/reject records just because a
report proposes it. A separate authorized evidence-backed update can apply
supported changes and log them. No new evidence is a valid finding. Never
create a recurring monitor unless the owner requested scheduling.
